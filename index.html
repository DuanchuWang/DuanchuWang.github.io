简介 About

我是西安交通大学博士生，研究方向为三维视觉与空间智能，包括点云理解、三维实例分割，以及基于激光雷达的精细森林结构分析。求学路径为武汉科技大学 → 西安电子科技大学 → 西安交通大学。

I am a Ph.D. student at Xi'an Jiaotong University. My research focuses on 3D vision and spatial intelligence, including point cloud understanding, 3D instance segmentation, and fine-grained forest structure analysis from LiDAR. I previously studied at Wuhan University of Science and Technology and Xidian University.
